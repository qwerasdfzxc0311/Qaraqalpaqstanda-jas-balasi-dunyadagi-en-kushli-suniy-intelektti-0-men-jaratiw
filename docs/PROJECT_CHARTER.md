# Project Charter

**Status:** Research and architecture design.  
**Implementation status:** No runtime implementation has been approved or delivered.

## Purpose

Qaraqalpaq is a long-term research and engineering effort to explore a capable, reliable artificial intelligence system that can perceive context, reason with evidence, plan, learn from experience, use permitted tools, monitor its own performance, and improve under controlled governance.

The project is not intended to be a static FAQ or a collection of prewritten responses. It aims to combine flexible AI behavior with traceable knowledge, explicit limits, and verifiable safety controls.

## Mission

Develop an AI system whose capabilities are demonstrated through reproducible evaluations, whose knowledge retains provenance and uncertainty, and whose actions remain within explicit human-authorized boundaries.

## Design principles

1. Evidence before claims.
2. Memory is information, not authority.
3. Source trust, epistemic status, and action permission are independent.
4. Preserve provenance, history, and unresolved contradictions.
5. Treat learning and self-improvement as controlled, measurable processes.
6. Use least privilege and require authorization for external effects.
7. Respect privacy, data rights, and applicable law.
8. Evaluate both safety and useful task performance.
9. Prefer reversible, observable, testable changes.
10. Do not claim consciousness, sentience, or AGI without an appropriate and independently reviewable basis.

## Non-goals at the current stage

- Claiming that Qaraqalpaq is conscious, sentient, or generally intelligent.
- Deploying an autonomous agent or connecting live external tools.
- Selecting a foundation model, database, vector store, cloud, GPU, or paid API before architecture and readiness review.
- Training on personal, private, or third-party data without rights and governance review.
- Allowing a system to grant itself permissions or rewrite production code.
- Treating biological inspiration as proof that a mechanism will work.

## Success criteria

Success is measured by documented, reproducible performance on defined tasks, robust handling of uncertainty and conflicting evidence, resistance to memory/tool misuse, preservation of privacy and authorization boundaries, and transparent reporting of limitations and resource costs.

## Governance

The canonical design and decision records live in the Qaraqalpaq Notion workspace. This repository is the version-controlled publication and implementation workspace for materials approved for release. Proposals must remain visibly distinct from accepted decisions and tested implementation.

## Current phase

Research synthesis, architecture definition, requirements, threat modeling, synthetic test design, and publication-readiness review. Runtime implementation remains gated.
